Privacy policy

Waqt – Prayer Times & Azan (Android app com.coderashraf.waqt and the Windows app). Effective 23 October 2026 (updated for version 1.9).

In short

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your data.
  • Prayer times, the Qibla direction, the Hijri and Bangla calendars, your prayer tracker, tasbih counts, Quran bookmarks, khatm goals, hifz progress, fasting and zakat records and settings are calculated and stored only on your device.
  • The app works offline. It uses the internet only for the optional features below.

These services receive your IP address as part of any internet request. Waqt does not attach any identifier to these requests, and nothing is stored by us. Their own privacy policies apply to how they handle requests.

Permissions (Android)

  • Approximate location — only while the app is open, to set your city and for travel mode (Qasr). Never in the background. You can type a city instead.
  • Notifications, Alarms & reminders — to play the adhan and reminders at the exact minute.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional) — to put the phone on vibrate during jamaat if you turn on auto-silent.
  • Run at startup — to restore prayer alarms after the phone restarts.

Backups

You can save a backup file of your data from Settings → Backup & restore, to a folder or cloud drive you choose. Waqt never uploads it anywhere itself. On Android, your data may also be included in your Google account's device backup if you have backup turned on; this is handled by Android, not by Waqt.

Children

Waqt is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/coderasraf/Waqt/issues.
